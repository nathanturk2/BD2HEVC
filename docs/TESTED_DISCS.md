# Tested Blu-ray collection

Snapshot: 30 September 2026. The maintainer's Celsus **Converted Backups** collection contains **117 eligible converted BD images**. The list below records the converted/test collection, including bonus discs and TV discs; it does not certify every menu, subtitle or edition.

3D Blu-rays and native UHD discs have been excluded. Older `(UHD converted)` names mean a standard Blu-ray reencoded to HEVC, not a native UHD disc or an upscale. No disc content or private storage paths are included here.
